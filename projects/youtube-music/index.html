Growth strategy + PRD · YouTube Music

Driving engagement in YouTube Music

WAU is the metric. The real question underneath it is which habit is worth building — because a streaming product does not lose users who dislike it, it loses users who never formed a reason to open it on a Tuesday.

Read the full PRD
FormatCase study plus a written PRD — problem statement, goals and non-goals, user stories, success metrics and acceptance criteria.
SurfaceYouTube Music — streaming, competing with Spotify at catalogue parity, where the differentiator has to be behavioural rather than editorial.
The questionWhich engagement loop actually produces a weekly open — and can social network effects do work that a recommendation algorithm can’t?
ArtifactThe PRD is the deliverable. It is linked above, unedited.

Weekly active is a habit metric wearing a usage metric’s clothes

When catalogue is at parity, nobody churns because the music is missing. They churn because the product never earned a recurring slot in the week. That reframes the work: the lever is not features, it is a repeatable reason to return that does not depend on the user remembering to.

How I worked it

  • Separated the WAU decline into its parts — new users who never formed a habit, and returning users whose trigger disappeared. They need different fixes.
  • Chose one loop rather than shipping several weak ones, since a habit that competes with itself is not a habit.
  • Wrote it as a PRD, including explicit non-goals — the fastest way to find out whether an idea is actually scoped is to try to write acceptance criteria for it.
