Trajectory · 2021 — present

Code, then product,
then AI platforms.

Not a straight line, but a consistent one. Every move traded a domain I understood for a harder problem, and kept the same underlying job: decide what a system should do when nobody has decided yet.

The engineering half

A CS degree specialised in ML and data science, then a year building and deploying models on Azure. It's why architecture reviews aren't a room I sit quietly in.

The product half

Three roles in a row where I owned the whole lifecycle: research, requirements, UX, delivery, and the client conversation when it slipped.

The through-line

Identity, satellites, LLM gateways — all systems where the interesting requirement is about failure modes, and where the honest answer to "what should this do?" starts as "nobody knows yet".

Four stops, in reverse.

Beacon.li Associate Product Manager
Oct 2025 — Jun 2026 Bengaluru · Hyderabad Enterprise AI platform
  • Owned product requirements and vendor integration strategy for a multi-provider LLM gateway across OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and Grok — defining fallback logic, error classification and retry behaviour to hold >85% request completion across enterprise workloads.
  • Owned the end-to-end redesign of the agent chat experience. Prototyped the streaming interface from scratch to decide what surfaces in real time, and designed a progressive-disclosure model that expands from an agent's answer into its reasoning rationale and then its raw tool-call traces — layered control over how deeply a user inspects agent behaviour.
  • Represented product in platform architecture reviews for a scalable agent orchestration system, making scoped configurability-vs-reliability trade-off calls that shaped routing design, tenant isolation boundaries and rollout sequencing.
  • Built a cost attribution framework mapping AI inference spend to tenant, agent and workflow level — the product foundation for usage-based pricing and client-facing budget governance.
  • Ran enterprise client touchpoints: structured feedback sessions, deployment pain points translated into specs, and expectations kept aligned with what was actually committed.
  • Took delivery ops from zero to a stable bi-monthly release cadence — 95% on-time delivery across 15 sprints — and cut concept-to-prototype turnaround by 60%+ by embedding Claude, Cursor and ChatGPT directly into PRD drafting, UX iteration and engineering handoff.
GalaxEye Space Associate Product Manager
